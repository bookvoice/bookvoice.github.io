No Right of Withdrawal for Digital Products

Policy regarding digital content supplied electronically, including eBooks, PDF files, audio files, and software.

Important: Due to the nature of digital products delivered electronically and made available immediately after purchase, the statutory right of withdrawal does not apply once performance has begun under the conditions set out by applicable law.

1. No Withdrawal After Performance Begins

Due to the nature of digital content that is not supplied on a tangible medium, all sales are final once the delivery or provision of access to the digital product has begun.

By completing a purchase, the customer expressly agrees that the digital content may be provided immediately and acknowledges that, by consenting to the start of performance before the expiry of any withdrawal period, any otherwise applicable statutory right of withdrawal is lost.

2. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to all digital products offered through this website or associated sales channels, including but not limited to eBooks, PDF documents, downloadable files, audio products, and software or software-related digital content.

3. Legal Basis

This policy is based on the legal rules applicable to digital content not supplied on a physical medium, including the consumer’s express consent to immediate performance and acknowledgment of the resulting loss of the right of withdrawal.

Reference to German law: The right of withdrawal expires in the case of a contract for the supply of digital content not on a tangible medium if the trader has begun performance of the contract after the consumer has expressly agreed that performance should begin before the withdrawal period expires and has confirmed knowledge that, by giving such consent, the consumer loses the right of withdrawal.

4. Finality of Sale

Once the digital product has been delivered, made available for download, transmitted electronically, or otherwise accessed by the customer, the contractual service is considered performed in full with respect to that digital content.
